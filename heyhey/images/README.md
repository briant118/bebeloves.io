# Our Anniversary Photos

This folder is for storing your couple photos. 

## How to add photos:
1. Add your photos to this folder
2. Name them: photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, photo3.jpg, photo4.jpg
3. The photos will automatically appear on the anniversary page

## Supported formats:
- JPG/JPEG
- PNG
- WebP

## Recommended photo sizes:
- Minimum: 300x300 pixels
- Optimal: 600x600 pixels or larger
- Aspect ratio: Square (1:1) works best

Enjoy your personalized anniversary page! 💕
